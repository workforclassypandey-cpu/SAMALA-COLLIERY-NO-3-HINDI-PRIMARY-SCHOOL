ABOUT OUR SCHOOL

SAMALA COLLIERY NO-03 HINDI PRIMARY SCHOOL WAS ESTABLISHED IN 1973.

THE SCHOOL HAS BEEN SERVING THE LOCAL COMMUNITY BY PROVIDING PRIMARY EDUCATION TO YOUNG CHILDREN.

THE SCHOOL PROVIDES EDUCATION FROM CLASS 1 TO CLASS 4.

THE SCHOOL FOCUSES ON BASIC EDUCATION, DISCIPLINE, GOOD HABITS, CONFIDENCE, KNOWLEDGE AND THE OVERALL DEVELOPMENT OF CHILDREN.

THE SCHOOL IS AN E.C.L AIDED PRIMARY SCHOOL LOCATED AT SAMALA, WEST BENGAL - 713346.

OUR AIM IS TO PROVIDE CHILDREN WITH A POSITIVE, SUPPORTIVE AND FRIENDLY LEARNING ENVIRONMENT.
