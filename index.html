Welcome to Samla Colliery No-03 Hindi Primary School

Samla Colliery No-03 Hindi Primary School is a primary school dedicated to providing quality basic education to children. The school has been serving the local community since 1973.

The school focuses on creating a friendly, disciplined and educational environment where young students can learn, grow and build a strong foundation for their future.

🏫 About Our School

Samla Colliery No-03 Hindi Primary School is a Hindi-medium primary school established in 1973.

The school provides primary-level education for young learners and helps children develop important academic, social and personal skills.

Students are encouraged to learn basic subjects, maintain discipline, participate in school activities and develop good values from an early age.

The school is located under the KHOTTADIH - PANDAVESWAR AREA and serves children from the surrounding community.

Our goal is to make education accessible to children and provide them with a strong foundation for their future.
